## Design Variation Rules:

**Layout Types (Rotate):**
1. Centered card
2. Split screen (left/right)
3. Diagonal split
4. Circular/Round
5. Fullscreen overlay
6. Grid-based
7. Timeline vertical
8. Dashboard panel
9. Magazine layout
10. App mobile style

**Animation Types:**
1. Fade & slide
2. Rotate/spin
3. Pulse/breathing
4. Float/bounce
5. Shake/wiggle
6. Zoom in/out
7. Flip/3D rotate
8. Glitch effect
9. Particle system
10. Morph/transform

**Color Schemes (Themes):**
- Dark mode: #000, #1a1a1a
- Light mode: #fff, #f5f5f5
- Vibrant: Bright saturated colors
- Pastel: Soft muted colors
- Neon: Glowing electric colors
- Monochrome: Single color variations
- Gradient: Multi-color blends
- Nature: Earth tones
- Tech: Blue/cyan
- Warm: Orange/red/yellow

This ensures EVERY design is truly unique!
